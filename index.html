Data deletion

You can delete data from the Threads bot section or request deletion of your data across all of the operator's tools that use the Meta app "Pavel Shumilin". This does not delete your Threads account or your original posts on Threads.

Delete data from the Threads bot section

  1. Open the dashboard and connect the account whose data you want to delete. The bot section is labelled "Бот Threads".
  2. Select "Delete bot data" (Удалить данные бота) and confirm.
  3. Wait for confirmation. Save the deletion status link, which remains available for 30 days.

This immediately deletes the stored access token, account ID, username and associated sessions. The application does not store post text or search results. The "Disconnect from bot" button (Отключить от бота) also deletes the stored connection and sessions, but does not create a separate deletion confirmation.

The "Delete bot data" button and its confirmation cover only the search section. They do not confirm deletion of analytics history or data from the separate personal pilot.

Request deletion across all tools

Email the operator at pavel@shumilin.ru with the subject "Data deletion request for the Pavel Shumilin app". Include your username and state that you want your data deleted from all tools that use this Meta app. The request covers the bot connection, analytics, the personal pilot, working copies associated with your account and backup archives.

After verifying that the account belongs to you, the operator stops further processing of its data, deletes the associated data, checks copies and confirms what has been deleted. If any part of the deletion is still pending, the operator will tell you separately. Do not send your password, access token or documents.

Revoke access in Threads

Removing the app in the Threads settings for connected apps revokes its access through Meta. To delete data already stored across all tools, also send the operator a request for deletion across all tools. The automatic confirmation from the search section does not replace this check.

If you cannot connect your account, email the operator at pavel@shumilin.ru. Include your username and a request to delete your data. Do not send your password, access token or documents. The operator will explain how to verify account ownership safely.

Operator: Individual Entrepreneur Pavel Sergeevich Shumilin. Read more about data processing.
