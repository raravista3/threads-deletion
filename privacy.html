Privacy policy

Version dated 7 November 2026

This policy covers the operator's tools connected to the Meta app "Pavel Shumilin": the Threads bot section, social media analytics and a personal pilot for approving posts. The bot section is available in the dashboard. Operator: Individual Entrepreneur Pavel Sergeevich Shumilin. For questions about data processing or deletion, email pavel@shumilin.ru.

Threads bot section

This section displays posts from the connected account and searches through the official Threads API. The dashboard is available to the owner. Authorised Meta reviewers have restricted access to the Threads bot section and cannot access the owner's analytics. The scope of search depends on Meta permissions. Until those permissions are approved, the application displays only the connected account's own posts.

Data processed

When you connect an account, Meta provides its account ID, username and an access token with a limited validity period. The application stores these in an encrypted file on the server. Your Threads password is not sent to the application.

To display posts and perform searches, the application temporarily receives post text, author names, publication times, links and media information. Post text, search queries and results are not written to the application's storage. They are processed in memory during the request and displayed in your browser.

The application sets a necessary session cookie for up to 24 hours. It links the browser to the connected account and protects actions. The dashboard uses no analytics or advertising cookies. The server receives technical connection data, including the IP address. The section itself does not log search phrases, results or authorisation parameters. The dashboard's shared web server logs the IP address, time, method, path and status of each request without the query string. Logs are rotated daily, with up to 14 archives retained. A separate necessary cookie, valid for up to 12 hours, is used to sign in to the dashboard.

Where data is sent

Authorisation and requests are handled by Meta's servers. Search phrases are sent to the Threads API. When an image or video is displayed, the browser loads the file from Meta's media server, which receives technical data about that connection. Opening an original post takes you to Threads. Meta's own data processing is governed by its terms and policies.

The Threads bot section does not send posts or account information to language models, Telegram or advertising systems. The section and the analytics are hosted on a Selectel server in Russia. Administrator access is needed to operate the service and handle deletion requests. This policy must be updated before other processors are added.

Retention and deletion

The access token and account information are stored until disconnection, deletion or token expiry, but for no longer than 60 days after connection. Session information is stored for up to 24 hours. Expired records are deleted at startup and within one minute while the application is running. Disconnecting or deleting data immediately removes account data and associated sessions from active storage.

After deletion, a random confirmation number without the account ID, the time and the operation status are retained for up to 30 days. This version does not create backups of the application's storage. Deleting dashboard data does not delete posts, the Threads account or data held by other applications.

Social media analytics

Analytics uses a separate connection to the same Meta app. It stores account information, post text and links, dates, media links, account and post metrics, measurement history, imported reports and the owner's notes. Instagram cover images are stored separately. These data are used to compare promotion results. Access tokens are encrypted. Ordinary database records are not encrypted at the application level. Access to analytics is protected by the owner's login.

Analytics history does not yet have an automatic deletion period. The 93-day collection window does not mean that older history is deleted. Disconnecting a source deletes its tokens but retains previously collected metrics and posts. The daily backup process keeps the latest seven archives of the database and encryption key. Separate imported and migration copies are checked when full deletion is requested.

Personal pilot for approving posts

The earlier personal pilot ran separately from the dashboard section. It stored drafts, explanations accompanying them, source posts and links, revision and approval history, publication results and operational records. Cards containing text and context were sent to the owner's private chat through the Telegram Bot API. Codex was also used to prepare materials. These recipients were part of the pilot's separate workflow. The dashboard section does not make these transfers.

Pilot files are protected by file permissions but are not encrypted by the application itself. Drafts and history have no automatic deletion period. Deletion on request covers the pilot's working files, related messages and available working copies, and includes requests to external processors. This description does not mean that Meta has approved data transfers to these recipients.

Your choices

You can disconnect your account, delete data in the dashboard, revoke access in Threads settings or contact the operator at pavel@shumilin.ru. Do not send your password, access token or copies of documents. Include your account username and describe your request. Verifying account ownership may require you to connect the account.
